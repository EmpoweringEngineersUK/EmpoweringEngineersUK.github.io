The Four Fundamental Principles

Engineering ethics is not just about avoiding illegal acts; it is about active commitment to the public good. This tool is calibrated against the Royal Academy of Engineering's Statement of Ethical Principles, which all UK registered engineers must uphold.

1. Honesty and Integrity
Engineers must be reliable and trustworthy. You must declare conflicts of interest and reject bribery or corrupt practices immediately.
2. Respect for Life, Law & Public Good
Health and safety is paramount. You have a duty to minimize potential harm to people and the environment, even if it conflicts with commercial goals.
3. Accuracy and Rigour
Act only within your area of competence. Do not mislead others about engineering data or results.
4. Leadership and Communication
Promote public awareness of the impact of engineering and listen to the concerns of society.
