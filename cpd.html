What is Continuing Professional Development (CPD)?

Continuing Professional Development (CPD) is the ongoing process of tracking and documenting the skills, knowledge, and experience you gain as you work. It is much more than just a list of training courses you have attended. Instead, your CPD log is a complete record of how you apply your learning to real-world tasks on site. This master hub brings together tools to help you manage your career progression. It breaks your learning down into clear targets, making it easy to see where your skills are strong and where you need improvement.

Why Does CPD Tracking Matter to Your Engineering Career?

When you apply for professional registration as an Engineering Technician (EngTech), Incorporated Engineer (IEng), or Chartered Engineer (CEng), you are judged against strict standards. The Engineering Council requires all registered engineers to maintain an up-to-date development record. This requirement is audited under UK-SPEC Competence E, which focuses on your personal commitment to professional standards. Many talented young technicians face unexpected delays in their career simply because they fail to log their learning.

A senior supervisor or a professional review panel wants to see that you take active responsibility for your development. By structured planning, you show that you care about keeping your skills relevant and your projects safe. Elite engineering firms organise their training around the balanced 70/20/10 Learning Model. This standard shows that 70% of your knowledge comes from practical, on-the-job experience. Another 20% comes from social learning, such as working with a mentor or peer reviews. The final 10% comes from formal education, like technical seminars or safety certifications. Recording these details proves you are ready for greater technical responsibility and leadership positions.
