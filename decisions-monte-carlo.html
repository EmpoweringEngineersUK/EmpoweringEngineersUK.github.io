What is a Monte Carlo Simulation?

A Monte Carlo simulation is a mathematical tool used to estimate risk and predict project outcomes under uncertain conditions. In traditional project planning, teams often guess a single fixed value for financial costs or schedule timelines. However, real-world engineering tasks are rarely that simple. Material delivery dates change constantly due to global supply chain pressures, on-site weather conditions delay construction loops unexpectedly, and commodity prices fluctuate wildly in the procurement market. A Monte Carlo simulation solves this exact problem by running thousands of automated calculations in rapid succession. It uses a range of possible values for each unknown factor instead of relying on a single optimistic number. The tool then combines all these structural variations to create a clear probability distribution curve. This curve displays every possible project outcome alongside its exact statistical likelihood of occurring, completely removing guesswork from your commercial planning loops.

Using a Monte Carlo simulation proves to review panels and senior executives that you possess strong financial literacy.
