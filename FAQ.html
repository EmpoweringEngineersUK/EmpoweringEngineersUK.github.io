Platform Help and Frequently Asked Questions

Starting your journey to become a Chartered Engineer (CEng), Incorporated Engineer (IEng), or Engineering Technician (EngTech) is a massive step. It is completely normal to have questions about the process. We built the Empowering Engineers UK platform to make your professional registration as simple as possible. This page is your central help desk. It explains how to use our tools safely and how to fix common problems.

Understanding Your Platform Journey

When you first start using a new digital tool, it can feel a little confusing. We understand that moving away from old spreadsheets and messy folders takes time. To help you feel confident, we have mapped out the four stages of using our platform:

  • Discover Phase: You might be wondering why you should use this platform. The answer is simple. Our tools do the hard work of organising your evidence, so you can focus on writing great engineering stories.
  • Envision Phase: Next, you will see how our tools fit together. For example, you will notice how the STAR Methodology framework automatically helps you meet the UK-SPEC communication rules.
  • Onboard Phase: This is where you start building your portfolio. You can safely type your notes directly into our secure tools and save your progress to your own computer.
  • Drive Value Phase: Finally, you will export your finished reports. You will have a clear, professional document ready for your Professional Review Interview (PRI).

How to Find the Answers You Need

If you get stuck, do not worry. We have built a smart search engine below to answer your questions instantly. Simply type a keyword like "Export", "Save", or "CEng" into the search box. The system will immediately filter the list to show you the exact steps you need to take.

Our platform uses secure local browser storage. This means your private company data stays entirely on your computer. Because of this high-level security, you must remember to manually download your backup files using the buttons at the bottom of the page. If you clear your internet history without saving, your work will be lost. Read through the questions below to learn exactly how to manage your files, change your screen colours, and prepare for your engineering interview.
