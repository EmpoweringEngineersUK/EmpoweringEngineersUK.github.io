🔒 DATA PRIVACY & INTEGRITY

Your data never leaves your device.
We use Local Storage technology, meaning your sensitive project details, STAR drafts, and matrix data are saved only in your browser's cache. We do not host a database. You can wipe your data instantly using the "Reset" buttons found in every tool.

Ethical AI Use:
Use these tools to structure and polish your genuine experience, not to fabricate it. The Engineering Council requires all evidence to be truthful. Using AI to invent scenarios is a breach of the Code of Conduct.
