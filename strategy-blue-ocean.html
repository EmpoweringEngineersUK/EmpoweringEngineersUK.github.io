Strategic Engineering Leadership: Applying Blue Ocean Strategy

When engineering companies bid for new projects, they often fight in overcrowded markets known as "Red Oceans." In these environments, firms battle aggressively over identical procurement frameworks, cutting prices, taking on dangerous contract risks, and accepting tiny profit margins just to win the job. But as you move into engineering management and aim for your Chartered Engineer (CEng) or Incorporated Engineer (IEng) registration, the Engineering Council expects more. Under the UK-SPEC guidelines, Competence C and Competence E require you to show advanced commercial leadership and continuous improvement. You must prove you can identify new ways to add value without destroying your project budget.

The Blue Ocean Strategy helps you break out of this trap. Instead of fighting competitors over the exact same technical delivery, it teaches you to execute "Value Innovation." This means you lower your costs while simultaneously creating new technical features that your clients actually care about, opening up clear, uncontested "Blue Oceans."

The ERRC Grid Explained

To achieve Value Innovation, engineering managers use the ERRC Grid (Eliminate, Reduce, Raise, Create). This forces your team to challenge standard industry habits:

  • Eliminate [E]: Remove costly legacy activities that add no real value. For example, eliminate physical paper drawing handovers entirely by enforcing a 100% digital cloud-based workflow.
  • Reduce [R]: Shrink technical parameters that are over-engineered. For instance, reduce excessive material safety margins that violate your carbon-reduction targets, while ensuring the design remains completely structurally sound.
  • Raise [Ra]: Elevate the quality of critical features well above the industry standard. For example, raise your statutory safety compliance transparency under the Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 (CDM) by giving clients real-time dashboards.
  • Create [C]: Invent completely new technical solutions. For example, create an automated predictive maintenance sensor package that the sector has never seen before, allowing you to charge a premium.

During your Professional Review Interview (PRI), the panel will ask how you handle commercial strategy. Showing them a completed ERRC Grid proves that you think like an executive director and can lead a department successfully.
