Privacy Policy & Data Sovereignty

At Empowering Engineers UK, safeguarding your professional intellectual property and personal privacy is a foundational operational directive. This comprehensive Privacy Policy delineates our rigid data handling protocols, telemetry tracking integrations, and the zero-knowledge architectural design of our application suite. By accessing and utilising our digital workbenches, you consent to the practices outlined within this framework.

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Local Storage Protocol

CORE DIRECTIVE: We absolutely do not store, parse, or transmit your engineering competence drafts on our external servers.

Engineering candidates—particularly those pursuing Chartered Engineer (CEng) or Incorporated Engineer (IEng) status—regularly process highly sensitive, commercially confidential project data to formulate their UK-SPEC competence reports. To completely mitigate the risk of data breaches or non-disclosure agreement (NDA) violations, this platform operates on a strict "Zero-Knowledge" client-side architecture.

When you input text into the STAR Methodology Architect, configure a Development Action Plan within the CPD Smart-Planner, or establish an Acronym Decoder database array, your data is sandboxed securely within your local device's browser memory cache using native Web Storage APIs. We have no mechanism to view or monetise your professional engineering drafts. Consequently, you retain absolute data sovereignty and are solely responsible for exporting and backing up your local payloads using the provided JSON extraction tools.

2. Telemetry and Analytical Tracking

To dynamically optimise our platform's layout algorithms and categorise popular coaching frameworks, we deploy Google Analytics 4 (GA4) telemetry. This system captures aggregated, anonymised data sets regarding user session durations, page routing interactions, and geographical access vectors. This analytical tracking strictly isolates behavioural trends and does not profile personal, identifiable user identities. IP anonymisation protocols are active by default to further insulate your systemic digital footprint.

3. Contextual Monetisation and Google AdSense

Empowering Engineers UK is maintained as a free, open-access platform to democratise professional registration resources. To offset hosting architectures and development overheads, we utilise the Google AdSense network to inject targeted B2B and technical advertisements into specific container slots across the website.

Google, as a third-party vendor, deploys tracking cookies to serve these advertisements based on your prior browsing history across the internet. These cookies enable Google and its integrated partner networks to deliver highly relevant commercial messaging. You maintain the right to opt out of personalised advertising at any time by configuring your preferences directly via the Google Ads Settings terminal.

4. Your Rights Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR framework, you are guaranteed specific rights regarding the processing of your personal data. Because Empowering Engineers UK fundamentally rejects the establishment of user accounts and does not capture your core technical inputs on external servers, the traditional mechanisms for backend data retrieval or erasure are decentralised. You exercise your right to erasure simply by executing a cache clearance protocol within your native browser settings, instantly wiping all traces of your engagement from your local hardware.

5. External Linkages and Third-Party Frameworks

Our coaching toolkits occasionally reference external institutional bodies, including the Engineering Council, the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET), and the Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE). We hold no jurisdiction over the privacy architectures deployed by these external domains. We strongly advise reviewing their respective security policies upon initialising an outbound connection.
