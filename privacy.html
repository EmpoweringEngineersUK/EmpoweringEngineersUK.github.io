At Empowering Engineers UK, safeguarding your professional intellectual property and personal privacy is a core operational requirement. This comprehensive Privacy Policy outlines our rigid data handling protocols, telemetry tracking integrations, and the zero-knowledge architectural design of our application suite. As an engineering professional, your project histories and career timelines are intensely valuable. This document forms a strict agreement describing exactly how our platform interacts with your information, ensuring you maintain absolute control over your notes at all times.

1. Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Local Storage Protocol

CORE DIRECTIVE: We absolutely do not store, parse, or transmit your engineering competence drafts on our external servers.

Engineering candidates—particularly those pursuing Chartered Engineer (CEng), Incorporated Engineer (IEng), or Engineering Technician (EngTech) status—regularly process highly sensitive, commercially confidential project data to formulate their UK-SPEC competence reports. A single accidental data leak could violate your company's Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs) and severely damage your professional reputation. To completely eliminate this risk, this platform operates on a strict Zero-Knowledge client-side framework.

When you input text into the STAR Methodology Architect, configure a Development Action Plan within the CPD Smart-Planner, or establish an Acronym Decoder database array, your text data is sandboxed securely within your local device's browser memory cache using native Web Storage APIs. We have no mechanism to view, copy, or monetise your professional engineering drafts. Consequently, you retain absolute data sovereignty and are solely responsible for exporting and backing up your local payloads using the integrated JSON extraction tools provided on the platform.

2. Telemetry and Analytical Tracking

To dynamically optimise our platform's layout algorithms, monitor traffic growth, and categorise popular coaching frameworks, we deploy standard Google Analytics 4 (GA4) telemetry. This system captures aggregated, anonymised data sets regarding user session durations, page routing interactions, and general geographical access vectors. This analytical tracking isolates behavioural trends to help us improve user flows and does not profile personal, identifiable user identities. IP anonymisation protocols are active by default across our scripts to further insulate your digital footprint. You do not need to perform any configuration steps to keep your identity secure; our systems handle this tracking inside isolated analytics blocks.

3. Contextual Monetisation and Google AdSense

Empowering Engineers UK is maintained as a free, open-access platform to democratise professional registration resources for early-career technicians. To offset hosting architectures, code maintenance, and development overheads, we utilise the Google AdSense network to inject targeted B2B and technical advertisements into specific container slots across the website. These slots ensure we can fund the platform without charging subscription fees.

Google, as a third-party vendor, deploys tracking cookies to serve these advertisements based on your prior browsing history across the internet. These cookies enable Google and its integrated partner networks to deliver relevant commercial messaging. You maintain the right to opt out of personalised advertising at any time by configuring your preferences directly via the official Google Ads Settings terminal.

4. Your Rights Under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR)

Under the UK GDPR framework, you are guaranteed specific rights regarding the processing, verification, and deletion of your personal data. Because Empowering Engineers UK fundamentally rejects the establishment of user accounts and does not capture your core technical inputs on external cloud servers, the traditional mechanisms for backend data retrieval or erasure are entirely decentralised. You can exercise your right to erasure instantly by executing a cache clearance protocol within your native browser settings, wiping all traces of your application segments from your local hardware storage.

5. External Linkages and Third-Party Frameworks

Our coaching toolkits occasionally reference external institutional bodies, including the Engineering Council, the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET), and the Institution of Mechanical Engineers (IMechE). We hold no jurisdiction over the privacy architectures deployed by these external domains. We strongly advise reviewing their respective security policies upon initialising an outbound connection to their portals.
