Professional Engineering Standards

Empowering Engineers UK is an open-access platform designed to democratise professional registration. We believe that lack of formal mentorship should not be a barrier to achieving Engineering Technician (EngTech), Incorporated Engineer (IEng) or Chartered Engineer (CEng) status.

Privacy & Data Integrity

We utilise Local Storage technology. This ensures your sensitive project data never leave your browser. No data is stored on our servers.
